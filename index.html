03 / Movement verification

Thrusters

Hip crease passes below the top of the kneecap. Finish with hips, knees, and arms fully locked overhead. No pause or jerk between squat and press.

Burpee broad jumps

Chest and thighs touch the floor. Two-foot takeoff and landing are required. Continue forward until the full 50-meter target is crossed.

Devil press

Chest and hips reach the floor between the dumbbells. Move the weights from floor to overhead in one continuous arc and finish in controlled, full lockout.

Row

Use a standard Concept2 with the monitor visible. Damper setting is athlete choice. Keep hands on the handle until the monitor reaches 1,000 meters.

Walking lunges

Each alternating forward step counts as one rep. Trailing knee makes clear floor contact each stride. Elite athletes hold dumbbells at their sides; drops pause forward progress until the load is lifted.

Scoring & eligibility

One continuous elapsed time starts at the Run 01 kickoff and stops at lockout of the final 100th lunge step. Ranked leaderboard entries require every movement to meet standard; no ranked scaling.
