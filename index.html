03 // EXECUTION & VERIFICATION

STRICT MOVEMENT STANDARDS

Zero compromises. Failure to lock out or complete specified depth results in an unverified rep or disqualification from global ranking.

Depth Rule: Hip crease must explicitly pass below the top of the kneecap at the bottom of the squat on every single rep.

Lockout Standard: Dumbbells must reach full extension overhead with hips, knees, and arms fully locked out in a straight vertical column.

Fluidity: No pausing or jerking movements between the squat phase and overhead press phase.

Chest Contact: Chest and thighs must completely touch the ground at the bottom of every single rep.

The Jump: Two-foot take-off and two-foot landing required. No stepping or skipping forward out of the burpee.

Distance: Athlete tracks forward continuously until the total target distance line of 50 meters is passed.

Burpee Phase: Hips and chest flat to the floor between the handles of the dumbbells.

Snatch Arc: Dumbbells must be ripped directly from the floor overhead in one continuous, fluid arc.

Overhead Lockout: Rep counts only when arms are fully locked out overhead with weights under full bodily control.

Equipment: Standard Concept2 rower with monitor clearly visible on submission video.

Calibration: Flywheel dampener is athlete choice. Monitor must count down or up to precisely 1,000 meters.

Handle Rule: Hands must remain on the handle until the monitor explicitly displays target completion.

Step Metric: 1 alternating forward step equals 1 counted rep. Total length equals exactly 100 steps.

Knee Contact: Trailing knee must make light, clear contact with the floor on every stride.

Farmer Carry (Elite): Dumbbells must be held strictly at sides. Drops permitted but forward progress is frozen until load is re-lifted.
